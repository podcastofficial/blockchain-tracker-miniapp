DEVELOPER / EVENT MONITORING

Smart Contracts

Add an EVM contract and every new transaction on it appears here live and is sent to your Telegram bot. Ownership, upgrade, pause and role changes are flagged separately.

ContractNetworkAddressTypeMonitoringActions
⌘
No contracts registeredAdd a contract address. Monitoring starts automatically; tap History to see its past transactions.
Live Contract Activity
New transactions on your contracts (refreshes every 30s)
Add a contract to see its activity here.
Alerts cover every transaction that emits an event on the contract (transfers, swaps, deposits, calls). Plain calls that emit no event are not detected; use History for those. Privileged-action detection covers standard events only: Ownable ownership transfer, EIP-1967 proxy upgrade and admin change, Pausable, and AccessControl role grant or revoke. Contracts with custom admin logic may not trigger alerts. Available on EVM networks. Each rule counts toward your alert limit, and the first scan only sets a starting point.
